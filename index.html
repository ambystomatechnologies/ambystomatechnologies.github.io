Cell Count (Axi IA)

Ambystoma Technologies · Smart laboratory support

Cell Count (Axi IA) multilingual is the definitive tool for students, researchers, brewers, and lab professionals designed to simplify cell counting and optimize the most common microbiological and hematological calculations. Developed by Ambystoma Technologies, it merges smart algorithms with an intuitive and smooth interface to offer accurate support in your everyday laboratory work.

📲 Download on Google Play Store

KEY FEATURES

🔬 Flexible Cell & Culture Counting
  • Counting on images: Upload or capture a microscope photo to count manually or algorithm-assisted.
  • Digital counting without image: Record counts on a virtual grid quickly and easily.
  • Measurement on images: Measure real distances and areas on your samples using a calibrated scale.
🍺 Specialized Brewery Tools
  • Yeast Counting & Viability: Easily classify yeast cells (live, dead, budding types A to H).
  • Neubauer Chamber Report: Perform quadrant counts, input dilution factors, and get viability and budding rates automatically.
  • Inoculation Calculation (Pitch Rates): Determine the exact amount of yeast required for your fermentation batch.
🩸 Clinical & Hematology Tools
  • Differential Leukocyte Count: Classify white blood cell types (neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, basophils).
  • Semi-quantitative Platelet Count: Estimate platelets on blood smears per oil immersion field.
  • Platelets & Erythrocytes in Neubauer Chamber: Automate cell concentration calculations in blood.
  • Bone Marrow Precursors & Reticulocytes: Record and evaluate cell maturation systematically.
  • Urinary Sediment Analysis: Record crystals, bacteria, and epithelial cells in urine samples.
🧪 Scientific & Laboratory Calculations
  • Most Probable Number (MPN): Estimate microorganism density in liquid samples with 3 and 5 tube series.
  • Smart Multi-timers: Manage simultaneous timers and alarms (with tube, flask, and petri dish animations) to never lose track of incubations and cultures.
  • Axi Chat: Ask technical or lab questions directly to our integrated assistant.
📋 Photo Logs & Professional Reports
  • Digital History: Save all your analyses and counts to review or edit them anytime.
  • Premium Reports: Export results in plain text (.txt) or as a ready-to-share high-quality image report (.jpg).
🔒 Absolute Privacy & Local Processing

100% Local & Secure: All image processing and analysis is performed entirely on your device. Your scientific samples and images are never uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

Biomedical Engineering & Open Source

Real-Time ECG Biomedical Monitor & Python DSP

Ambystoma Technologies · High-Fidelity Soundcard Acquisition & Adaptive Signal Filtering

The Real-Time Electrocardiogram (ECG) Monitor is an open-source non-invasive electrophysiology telemetry workstation developed by Ambystoma Technologies. Designed specifically in collaboration with cardiovascular research for real-time cardiac arrest analysis, this system overcomes the financial barriers of closed proprietary clinical acquisition software ($500 - $1,000 USD). It integrates an AD8232 analog front-end, high-sampling audio port acquisition, custom dry-contact 3D finger electrodes, and an adaptive digital signal processing (DSP) architecture in Python.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ENGINEERING INNOVATIONS

🎛️ High-Fidelity Analog Acquisition via Soundcard Interface (TRS 3.5 mm)

To eliminate baud-rate bottlenecks and digital ground noise inherent to USB-serial microcontroller communication, the preconditioned biopotential from the AD8232 is routed directly into the computer's analog audio port (Line-In / Microphone TRS). This leverages the soundcard's high-speed analog-to-digital converter (ADC) and ultra-low noise floor, eliminating the need for expensive external digital USB isolators ($40+ USD).

  • Baseline Stabilization: Arduino Nano inputs (D10, D11) provide stabilized reference voltage potentials and lead-off detection without injecting USB power-rail noise.
  • Instant Hardware Recognition: Fully integrated with standard OS audio subsystem (Line-In / Microphone channel at high sample rates).
🔋 Galvanic Decoupling & Autonomous DC Power

The telemetry module operates on an isolated DC battery pack, completely severing physical connection to the commercial AC electrical grid. This suppresses 50 Hz / 60 Hz mains harmonic induction and ground-loop common-mode currents, which are the primary sources of baseline wandering in clinical telemetry.

🖐️ Ergonomic Reusable 3D Dry-Contact Limb Electrodes

Eliminating disposable Ag/AgCl wet gel adhesive pads—which dry out, degrade signal impedance, and require invasive chest placement—we developed custom 3D-printed distal limb clamps with electrolytic copper conductive strips.

  • Einthoven Geometry: Placed on index and middle fingers to establish stable peripheral Leads I, II, and III with reference ground (earlobe or forearm).
  • Hygienic & Immediate: Rapid non-invasive application, zero skin irritation, washable, and instantly reusable across multiple research subjects.
🐍 Real-Time Python DSP Engine & Adaptive Filtering

Unlike heavy computing environments (such as MatLab/Simulink) which introduced critical 5-second sampling latencies unsuitable for acute cardiac arrest monitoring, our Python DSP pipeline runs with real-time response (<50 ms latency).

  • Adaptive Notch & Butterworth Filtering: Tunable cutoff frequencies (e.g., 35 Hz to suppress multi-source wave superposition / Young's interference patterns caused by nearby medical lamps, heating blankets, and ventilators).
  • Clinical Display Grid: 5-second observation window with calibrated 40 ms (0.04 s) small divisions for unequivocal P-Q-R-S-T-U complex evaluation.
  • Multispecies Dynamic Auto-Scaling: Automatic amplitude voltage scaling accommodating both human signals (~1 mV) and microvolt rodent electrophysiology (laboratory murine models).
🛡️ Multi-Tier Electromagnetic Shielding (Faraday Enclosure)

To safeguard high-impedance biopotential nodes in electronically crowded environments, the instrumentation incorporates balanced shielded coaxial cabling, an integrated grounded metallic mesh Faraday cage, and an internal RF reflective shield, insulating sensitive circuitry from ambient RF telemetry noise.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Analog Front-End (AFE) Analog Devices AD8232 (SparkFun Heart Rate Monitor) with integrated 2-pole high-pass and 3-pole low-pass filters
Reference Controller Arduino Nano (ATmega328P) for logic-level reference, baseline stabilization, and lead-off detection
Signal Acquisition Channel 3.5 mm TRS Analog Audio Interface (Line-In / Microphone ADC) delivering high sampling frequency and low noise floor
Power Isolation Autonomous DC Battery Pack (100% galvanic isolation from AC electrical grid and USB ground loops)
Biopotential Electrodes Reusable 3D-printed ergonomic distal clamps with high-conductivity copper strips (Einthoven peripheral Leads I, II, III)
DSP Pipeline & Filtering Real-time Python engine, dynamic adaptive notch filtering (35 Hz - 60 Hz), high-order Butterworth low-pass stage (<50 ms latency)
Clinical Display Standard High-contrast monochrome, 5.0 s sweep window, calibrated 40 ms (0.04 s) division grid, HD snapshot capture for research
Multispecies Compatibility Dynamic voltage auto-scaling: calibrated for human ECG (~1 mV) and microvolt murine electrophysiology (laboratory rats)
Shielding Architecture Balanced shielded audio cable, grounded metallic Faraday mesh cage, and secondary RF reflective barrier
